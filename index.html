Service Menu

Monette's Hair Services

Haircuts

  • Classic Bob - A timeless, polished cut that frames the face with a clean, even shape.
  • Lob / Long Bob - A versatile, shoulder-length bob that offers movement and easy styling.
  • Angled Bob - Shorter at the back and longer in front for a sleek, modern silhouette.
  • Stacked Bob - Graduated layers create beautiful volume and definition at the back.
  • Pixie Cut - A chic, low-maintenance short cut tailored to your face shape and style.
  • Layered Cut - Strategically placed layers add movement, dimension, and natural volume.
  • Shag Cut - Textured layers and effortless movement create a relaxed, modern look.
  • Blunt Cut - A sharp, one-length finish that makes hair appear fuller and healthier.
  • Curtain Bangs / Fringe Cut - Face-framing bangs customized to complement your features and hairstyle.
  • Undercut - A hidden or visible shaved section that reduces bulk or adds a bold edge.
  • U-Shape / V-Shape Cut - A beautifully shaped perimeter that maintains length while adding dimension.
  • Feathered Cut - Soft, flowing layers create lightness, movement, and a flattering finish.
  • Asymmetrical Cut - An artistic, uneven shape designed for a distinctive contemporary look.

Hair Coloring

  • Full Color - Refresh or transform your look with rich, even color from roots to ends.
  • Root Touch-Up - Cover regrowth and greys while maintaining your existing hair color.
  • Foil Highlights - Precisely placed highlights add brightness, contrast, and dimension.
  • Balayage - Hand-painted highlights create a soft, natural, sun-kissed finish.
  • Babylights - Ultra-fine highlights deliver delicate brightness and seamless dimension.
  • Lowlights - Deeper tones add richness, contrast, and natural-looking depth.
  • Ombré - A gradual transition from darker roots to noticeably lighter ends.
  • Sombré - A softer, more subtle version of ombré with seamless tonal blending.
  • Color Correction - A customized service that corrects unwanted tones, uneven color, or previous color concerns.
  • Fashion Colors - Express yourself with customized pastel, vivid, or creative shades.
  • Grey Blending - Softly blend greys into your natural color for a graceful, low-maintenance result.
  • Toner / Glaze - Refine your tone, neutralize brassiness, and add beautiful shine.

Styling

  • Blowout - Professional drying and styling for smoothness, volume, and lasting movement.
  • Iron Curls / Waves - Customized curls or waves ranging from soft and romantic to polished and glamorous.
  • Flat-Iron Straightening - A smooth, sleek finish with enhanced shine and frizz control.
  • Updos - Elegant, secure styling created for weddings, proms, and formal occasions.
  • Braided Styles - Beautifully crafted braids customized for casual, creative, or formal looks.
  • Special-Occasion Styling - Personalized hairstyling designed to complement your outfit, event, and individual style.

Treatments

  • Deep Conditioning - An intensive moisture treatment that restores softness, shine, and manageability.
  • Keratin / Smoothing Treatment - Reduce frizz and improve smoothness for easier, longer-lasting styling.
  • Scalp Treatment - Cleanse, exfoliate, and nourish the scalp to support a healthier hair environment.
  • Olaplex / Bond Repair - Strengthen damaged hair by repairing bonds affected by coloring, heat, and chemical services.
  • Hair Mask - A concentrated treatment customized to hydrate, strengthen, or revive stressed hair.

Texture Services

  • Perm - Create lasting curls, waves, movement, or volume customized to your desired look.
  • Relaxer - Professionally straighten or soften natural curls for a smoother, more manageable finish.
  • Texturizing - Adjust the natural curl pattern to reduce bulk and improve movement and manageability.

Extensions

  • Tape-In Extensions - Lightweight extensions applied with discreet adhesive tabs for natural-looking length and volume.
  • Clip-In Extensions - A flexible, temporary option for instantly adding length, volume, or color.
  • Sew-In / Weave - Extensions securely sewn onto braided hair for lasting fullness and versatility.
  • Fusion / Keratin-Bond Extensions - Individually bonded strands create seamless, natural-looking length and movement.

Bridal & Event Packages

  • Bridal Trial - Preview and perfect your wedding hairstyle through a personalized consultation and styling session.
  • Wedding-Day Styling - Beautiful, long-lasting bridal hairstyling completed with careful attention to every detail.
  • Bridal-Party Packages - Coordinated hairstyling for bridesmaids, mothers, and other members of your wedding party.
